# Community conduct

Status: proposed community policy, not yet an adopted external standard. Enforcement contact and final adoption remain pending before external participation opens. No named third-party code of conduct is claimed.

Participants should communicate respectfully, discuss technical disagreements on evidence, respect privacy and credit others' work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable.

Maintain privacy in examples and reports: do not expose seller identities, invoices, addresses, credentials or other private collection data. Prefer synthetic fixtures.

Once an enforcement contact is selected, reports should be handled privately and proportionately. The maintainer may request corrections or restrict participation to protect the community; concrete reporting and appeal arrangements must be documented before this draft becomes active policy.
